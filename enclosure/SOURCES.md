# Dimensional references and attribution

FlowSight Desk is an original enclosure built with CadQuery. The sources below were consulted to establish the board envelopes, mounting patterns and display areas. Their enclosure and stand designs were not used as the basis of this design.

| Reference | Dimensions used |
|---|---|
| [ESP32-2432S028 original structure diagram](https://github.com/witnessmenow/ESP32-Cheap-Yellow-Display/blob/main/OriginalDocumentation/3-Structure_Diagram/Dimensions.png) | Mounting hole pitch: 78 × 42 mm. The dimensions labelled in this diagram are the mounting pitch, not the outer PCB size. |
| [CYD reference CAD by Havenview](https://github.com/witnessmenow/ESP32-Cheap-Yellow-Display/blob/main/3dModels/Havenview_CYD_CAD/Cheap%20Yellow%20Display%20v16.step) | Board envelope measured from its board solid: 86.6 × 50 × 9.8 mm. The other solids were not incorporated. |
| [Inventech CYD product specification](https://www.inventech.co.za/shop/development-boards-1/cheap-yellow-display-esp32-50) | Nominal module size: 86 × 50 mm. |
| [ESP32-2432S024 structure diagram](https://github.com/jpduhen/CYD_2.4inch_ESP32-2432S024/blob/main/3-Structure_Diagram/Dimensions.png) | Mounting hole pitch: 68 × 36.5 mm. |
| [ESP32-2432S024 specification](https://github.com/jpduhen/CYD_2.4inch_ESP32-2432S024/blob/main/2-Specification/ESP32-2432S024%20Specifications-EN.pdf) | Module: 74.4 × 42.9 mm. Active display area: 48.96 × 36.72 mm. |

Board revisions can differ. Confirm the actual dimensions, display offset, component height and USB position with the fit template before assembling a complete enclosure. The CS-32 N4XX has confirmed 024-compatible wiring in this project; its mechanical dimensions have not been measured.

The official FlowSight logo is included as the branding source. Manrope is by the Manrope Project Authors and distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see [OFL.txt](source/OFL.txt). The Manrope Bold instance is generated from the bundled variable font. The demo screen is a framebuffer capture generated by this project's firmware using test data.

Digital verification results are recorded in [geometry-verification.json](geometry-verification.json). They cover mesh integrity and the modelled clearances; they do not establish physical fit, touch pressure, stability or temperature performance of a manufactured unit.
